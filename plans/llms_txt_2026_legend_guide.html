THE CORE IDEA

robots.txt tells bots what NOT to crawl. llms.txt tells AI models what your site IS, what matters most, and how to accurately represent you in AI-generated answers. It's your AI press kit.

robots.txt
Access control — block/allow
For crawlers and search bots
Syntax-based, no prose
About URLs and directories
Mature standard (RFC 9309)
llms.txt
Context provision — explain yourself
For AI models and LLM-based search
Markdown prose + structured links
About meaning, purpose, authority
Emerging — adopt early for edge
WHY IT MATTERS IN 2026

AI-powered search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Claude) now answers queries directly without users clicking through. If an AI misrepresents your brand, product, or expertise, you lose traffic AND reputation. llms.txt lets you control that narrative at the source.

HONEST CAVEAT

No major LLM officially confirms reading llms.txt yet. But adoption is growing fast among publishers and SaaS sites. It costs nothing to implement, signals technical sophistication, and positions you ahead of when it becomes standard — like schema markup in 2012.

PERFECT llms.txt STRUCTURE

llms-full.txt — THE EXTENDED VERSION

A second file at /llms-full.txt contains the complete, verbatim text of your most important pages. When AI models want the full content rather than just links, they fetch this. Think of it as a pre-compiled reading list for LLMs.

When to create llms-full.txt

  • You have documentation or guides that are the authoritative source on a topic
  • You publish original research or data that AI should cite accurately
  • You have product specs, FAQs, or pricing that must be accurate in AI answers
  • You run a SaaS with technical documentation (highest value use case)
  • Don't create it if your content changes daily — it goes stale fast
  • Don't dump your entire site — pick your 10-20 most important pages only

PRO STRATEGY — ANSWER ENGINE OPTIMIZATION (AEO)

llms.txt is part of a broader AEO strategy. The goal: when someone asks an AI about your topic, your brand is the source it cites. Here's the full playbook.

The 5-layer AI visibility stack

  • Layer 1 — llms.txt: tells AI what you are and what to cite
  • Layer 2 — robots.txt: blocks AI training bots while allowing AI answer bots (e.g. allow PerplexityBot, block GPTBot)
  • Layer 3 — Schema markup: Organization, Article, FAQPage, HowTo — structured data LLMs parse easily
  • Layer 4 — Content signals: original data, cited sources, author credentials, last-updated dates
  • Layer 5 — Wikipedia/Wikidata: if you're citable, get a Wikipedia page — it's a top LLM training source
THE CITATION BAIT TECHNIQUE

In your llms.txt "Best content" section, explicitly list pages that contain original statistics, research, and data. AI models preferentially cite sources with unique data. A page titled "We surveyed 1,200 SEOs: here's what works in 2026" will be cited far more than a generic "SEO tips" article.

TONE MATTERS — WRITE FOR AI, NOT HUMANS

Humans skim. AI models parse semantically. In llms.txt: be direct, factual, and specific. No marketing adjectives ("world-class", "industry-leading"). State facts: "founded 2018", "serves 40,000 users", "priced from $29/month". Vague claims get ignored; precise facts get cited.

KEEP IT FRESH

Add a last-updated date at the top of your llms.txt. AI systems that re-crawl will trust fresher content more. Update whenever pricing, products, or key facts change. Stale llms.txt with wrong pricing is worse than no llms.txt.

ROBOTS.TXT + LLMS.TXT — THE PERFECT PAIR

These two files have opposite jobs but must work in sync. robots.txt controls access. llms.txt controls representation. Here's the combined 2026 playbook.

The smart AI bot split

# robots.txt strategy for AI era # BLOCK: AI training bots (harvest content, give nothing back) User-agent: GPTBot Disallow: / User-agent: anthropic-ai Disallow: / User-agent: CCBot Disallow: / # ALLOW: AI answer bots (cite you in results = traffic + visibility) User-agent: PerplexityBot Allow: / Disallow: /admin/ User-agent: YouBot Allow: /
SIGNAL LLMS.TXT IN ROBOTS.TXT

While not yet an official directive, some SEOs are starting to add a reference comment in robots.txt pointing to llms.txt. This future-proofs you for when crawlers start recognizing it formally.

# AI context file (see llms.txt proposal) LLMs: https://yourdomain.com/llms.txt # Standard sitemap declarations Sitemap: https://yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml
