## Notes for Creative Team

- **Primary Text** — All entries are under 125 characters (Meta's recommended limit for optimal delivery). Use this as the main body copy.
- **Headline** — Keep bold; renders below the image/video on feed. Max ~40 chars. These are tight and scannable.
- **Description** — Use for secondary specs/trust signals. Appears below headline.
- **URGENT:** Stock levels are low (iPhone: 9, S23 Ultra: 15). Consider adding "Low Stock" badges on creatives for Ad Sets 1 & 4.
- **Suggested Creative:**
  - **Price-focused (1, 4, 7):** Product hero shot + price overlay with discount badge.
  - **Feature-focused (2, 5, 8):** Lifestyle / in-use shots — for the heating pad, show it worn discreetly under clothes or a woman working comfortably while wearing it.
  - **Trust-focused (3, 6, 9):** Review screenshot cards or user-generated content. For the heating pad (Ad 9), consider a testimonial-style video or static card with Megha's quote.
- **Audience targeting note:** Menstrual Heating Pad ads (7–9) should target women 18–45 in India. Consider excluding men to avoid wasted spend. Use interest targeting: "menstrual health," "period pain," "wellness," "women's health."
