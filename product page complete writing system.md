Complete guide to title and content structure for high-conversion Indian ecommerce product pages
1
The winning title formula
Every high-converting title follows this exact pattern — 4 parts, always in this order:
Result / outcome

- Product name
- Key spec or material
- Who it is for / fit
  Good title example
  "Stop oil burns — silicone splatter guard for all Indian kadais and pans, 28 cm, food-grade"
  Never start with brand name or model number. Customers search for their problem, not your brand.
  60–80 characters max (mobile cuts off at 65)
  First 5 words carry 80% of SEO weight
  Include the pain word (burns, leak, snore, stain…)
  Mention size, colour, count where relevant
  No ALL CAPS, no excessive punctuation
  No fake claims like "best ever" or "world no.1"
  2
  Subtitle / one-line tagline
  Sits directly below the title. One sentence. Amplifies the emotion the title sparked.
  Emotion word
- What life looks like after
- Time or effort saved
  Good tagline example
  "Cook your favourite dishes without the fear of oil burns — kitchen stays clean in seconds."
  Max 120 characters. Sentence case. No full stop needed.
  3
  Bullet benefits — the 5-point block
  Each bullet = one outcome + one proof. Never just a feature. Follow this structure for all 5 bullets:
  Bullet 1
  Core benefit
  The #1 reason they buy
  Bullet 2
  Material / safety
  Builds trust in quality
  Bullet 3
  Ease of use
  No learning curve
  Bullet 4
  Saves money / time
  ROI vs alternative
  Bullet 5
  Guarantee
  Return / warranty
  Written example (splatter guard)
  Zero oil splatter — fits over any pan or kadai up to 36 cm, blocks 99% of hot oil drops
  100% food-grade silicone — BIS certified, no harmful chemicals, safe for daily cooking
  Just rinse or dishwash — clean in 10 seconds, no soaking, no scrubbing needed
  Saves ₹800/year in stove cleaning — replaces paper towels and disposable covers
  7-day no-questions return — we collect from your door free if it does not work for you
  4
  Description body — the scroll section
  Build the description in 4 named sub-sections. Short paragraphs only — 2–3 lines each. Indian buyers scan, not read.
  The problem (empathy hook)
  Must have
  Open with the exact pain in plain language — no jargon
  Name the frustration the customer feels daily
  2–3 lines only. Make them feel understood before you sell.
  "Every time you make tadka or deep fry, hot oil flies everywhere — on your hands, the stove, the wall behind. You spend more time cleaning than cooking. And every time it happens, there is that moment of fear before it burns."
  The solution (product reveal)
  Must have
  Introduce the product as the direct answer to the pain above
  Explain how it works in one plain sentence — no technical jargon
  Then list what changes in the customer's daily life
  "This food-grade silicone guard sits over your pan and stops oil before it leaves the surface. Steam passes through the mesh — so your food cooks perfectly — while every oil drop stays inside. Your stove is clean. Your hands are safe."
  What's in the box + specs
  Must have
  List every item included — no surprises at delivery
  Key specs: size, material, weight, colour, compatibility
  Use a clean two-column spec table format
  1× silicone splatter guard · 1× storage ring · 1× care card
  Size: 28 cm · Material: BIS food-grade silicone · Heat safe: up to 230°C · Colour: black / grey
  Who this is perfect for
  Conversion boost
  Name 3–4 specific customer types — makes them self-identify
  Include gifting angles — huge for Indian market
  "Perfect for home cooks who fry daily · Working mothers who want faster cleanup · Anyone gifting for a housewarming or wedding · Hostel and PG kitchens with shared stovetops"
  5
  Words that convert vs words that kill trust
  Use these words
  Stop / end / never again
  Finally / now you can
  In seconds / in minutes
  Zero [pain word]
  Certified / tested / verified
  No-questions return
  Saves ₹[X] per month

  Best in world / #1 brand
  Revolutionary / breakthrough
  Premium quality (without proof)
  100% guaranteed results
  Limited time offer!!! (over-used)
  Innovative / next-gen / smart
  Cheap / low-cost / affordable

This is the complete writing system — use it as a checklist for every single one of your 20 products.
The single biggest mistake Indian sellers make is writing titles that start with the brand name or product category. Nobody searches "Brand X Splatter Guard." They search "stop oil burns while cooking" or "pan cover for frying." Your title must speak the customer's language, not yours.
The 4-part description structure works because it mirrors how a buying decision actually happens in the brain — first the customer needs to feel understood (problem hook), then curious about the fix (solution reveal), then confident in the quality (specs + what's in the box), and finally they need to see themselves in it (who it's for). Only after all four does the "add to cart" feel natural.
One thing specific to the Indian market worth noting: the "saves ₹X per month" angle in the bullets is extremely powerful. Indian buyers are value-conscious. Showing them that a ₹349 splatter guard saves ₹800/year in cleaning supplies and stove servicing makes the purchase feel like a smart financial decision, not an expense.
